# What I see in the references

Screenshots are pixels. Notes are rules. Write what you actually see, not adjectives.

## Type
- <Family, weight, size of display vs body, tracking, leading>

## Spacing
- <Grid, margins, section gaps, how many things per screen>

## Images
- <Photo style, grade, corners, crops>

## Colour
- <Ground, text, the only accent and where it is allowed>

## Details worth taking
- <Buttons, numerals, footer, motion: the specific moves you want>

## AVOID
Gradients, glow, emoji icons, fake logos, fake stats, testimonials,
"Trusted by", rating badges, "make it pop".
