# What I see in the references (Syed Saboor's editorial work)

## Type
- One grotesk family (Geist). Display huge but regular weight (~400-500), tight tracking, leading ~0.9.
- Two sizes do the work: huge and small (body 16-17px). Section heads used sparingly.
- No serif, no italic accent word, no mono labels as decoration.

## Spacing
- 12-column grid, wide equal outer margins, tall section gaps.
- One dominant thing per screen (a wordmark, a photo, a numeral) plus a little small text.
- Columns align top and bottom. No staggered card columns.

## Images
- One art-directed photo series, one grade (warm-neutral, slightly desaturated, soft light).
- Square corners. Full-bleed in the hero, the wordmark over the lower third.

## Colour
- Bone ground + ink text, one tone step for panels. Photos carry the warmth.
- The only hue on the page: a semantic green for "approved" inside the product UI.

## Details worth taking
- CTA pair: solid pill + underlined text link with an arrow. Repeat it at every decision point.
- Big numerals in ruled cells with tiny lowercase captions (Almare).
- The footer as a designed object: big trial cell, link cells with ↗, giant wordmark (Sigil).
- Calm motion: soft rise reveals, a slow marquee, count-up numerals. No bounce.

## AVOID
Gradients, glow, emoji icons, fake logos, fake stats, testimonials,
"Trusted by", rating badges, purple/blue SaaS glow (x_analytics_a.jpg is
in here as the counter-example), "make it pop".
