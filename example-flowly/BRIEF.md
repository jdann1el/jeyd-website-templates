# Flowly — landing page brief

## What it is
Flowly is a B2B SaaS that turns a team's requests and approvals into flows
that run on their own. You describe a process in plain words, Flowly drafts
the flow, you check it, and it runs every time.

## Who it's for
Operations, finance and people teams at companies of 20 to 500 people.
Buyer: the head of operations. Users: everyone who sends or approves requests.

## The one action
Start free (14-day Team trial, no card needed).

## The one idea
One approval, travelling. Marta asks for a new vendor; Jordi approves it
from his phone. That message appears in the hero, gets drafted in the
builder, arrives in Slack / Teams / email, and ends as lines in the run log.

## Facts (use only these)
- Flow builder from plain words: write the process as a sentence; every step stays editable.
- Approvals in Slack, Microsoft Teams or email with Approve / Reject. Reminder after 24 h, escalation after 48 h.
- 60+ connectors (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, Google Sheets, Notion, Jira,
  HubSpot, Salesforce, NetSuite, Holded, BambooHR, Personio). Every run in an audit log.
- Example flows: new vendor request, employee onboarding, expense over €500.
- Pricing (EUR, monthly; annual saves 2 months):
  Free €0 (3 active flows, 500 runs/month, 5 users);
  Team €12/user/month (unlimited flows, 10,000 runs/month, all connectors);
  Business €29/user/month (100,000 runs/month, SSO, audit log export, EU data residency).
- Founded 2024 in Barcelona. Data hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). GDPR-compliant.
  SOC 2 Type II audit in progress (NOT certified yet).
- Approvers who only answer from Slack, Teams or email are not billed.
- Flowly is fictional (made for a Jeyd episode). Say so in the footer.
